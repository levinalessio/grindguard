# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help people who struggle with focus. Many of them will show up here. Be decent to them.

- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, slurs, sexualized content, threats or personal attacks.
- People here talk openly about procrastination, attention problems and bad habits. Do not mock that.
- Do not post anyone's private information. That includes browsing histories, screenshots of other people's tabs and real names they did not share themselves.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close discussions and block people who break this, in public spaces of the project and in private messages about it.

To report something, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

The spirit of this document follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), just shorter.
